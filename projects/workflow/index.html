SaaS / Operations management

Workflow

A SaaS for small agencies to keep clients, projects, deliverables and tasks inside one operational hierarchy.

Open live project ↗Next case →
Role
Full-stack product project
Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Vitest, Playwright
Outcome
Read-only demo with server-side authorization and automated tests

Context

Small agencies can lose deadlines and approvals when client work is scattered across disconnected tools. The product brings the operational chain into one place, so the next decision is visible instead of buried in a generic dashboard.

Product decision

I connected clients, projects, deliverables and tasks in one hierarchy, then designed the attention center around signals that need action. The demo uses fictitious data and remains safe to inspect publicly.
